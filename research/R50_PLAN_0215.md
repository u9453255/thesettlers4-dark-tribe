## 2. Where things stand after r49

The economy side is sound in pid 18584: keep 34 to 47 with the kingdom, carriers never pinned (29..75, median 54), builders and diggers back within 2,000 ticks after each of three mass losses, iron ore from tick 12,500, toolsmith output 5 to 7 of 8, 16 campaigns and 25 collapsed Dark buildings, soldiers 96 to 264 at the peak. The weak side is the gardener supply and the land defence.

Still failing, with the numbers behind each:

2. The r49 drop took the last piece of a kind twice (an axe at 25,020, a hammer at 26,770).
3. A full output with one lying shovel jams forever: the drop spares the shovel, recruitment refuses it (free shovels at or below the reserve). pid 31460 ended in that state (pickaxes 2, rods 3, scythes 2, shovel 1, order 4 pending, `no spare shovels` 24 times).

5. A rise of `SeriousRise` 40 cells or more per scan still waits out `LandDefendGap` 12,000 (3 deferrals in 18584, 18 in 31460, one at tick 130,510 with a rise of 396).
6. A defence follows any seen units: 111 soldiers and 1 gardener went 118 tiles to two Dark Gardeners at (389,240) while own Dark rose 2,207 to 3,961 elsewhere (31460, tick 131,430).
7. A campaign holds every gardener, so own Dark rose 1,967 to 3,567 during one raid (93,940..105,560) and 3,253 to 4,636 during one sweep (109,920..121,940) in 18584.
8. Unproven: the fire keep (`HuntKeep` 42) may hide Dark units near the Temple so that a threat looks land-only; the ledger counts 40 gardeners at the end of 18584 while recruitment sees 18..19 within `LaunchReach` 130 of home.
9. The report calls the r47 room a failure ("reserve below keep" 43 times) because the room is not in the telemetry.

## 3. Fix 1: faster gardener replacement

Problem: the plugin keeps `ShovelReserve` 2 free shovels for the AI's diggers and orders `ShovelBatch` 4 at a time, so a replacement gardener needs one full toolsmith cycle each. Since r47 the diggers are protected by the reserve room, not by spare shovels.

Change:
- `ShovelReserve` 2 -> 1, `ShovelBatch` 4 -> 6 (`native/offence-model.hpp`, the `GardenersWanted` line and the `ShovelBatch` line). Recruitment then proceeds while 2 or more free shovels exist; the order tops up to 6 missing shovels at a time.
- `recruitOrder`, `shovelTarget`, `shovelOrder` and the per-tick restore of the order byte (r29 `ShovelWatch`) stay as they are.

Code: `native/offence-model.hpp` (constants), `tools/inspect-offence.py` (the constant strings of the r29 block).

Tests (`native/offence-tests.cpp`, `toolsmithShovels`): the wait reason flips from "no spare shovels" to a recruit at 2 free shovels; an order never exceeds 6 on top of the existing one; a native clear is still restored.

Risk: a batch of 6 costs 6 iron bars and 6 coal while gardeners are missing; the order stops at 6 and recruits take the shovels, so the output cannot fill with them. The AI's own digger orders are unaffected.

## 4. Fix 2: the drop rule

Problem: `dropCandidate` (r49) may take a pile from 1 to 0, and it never acts while a free shovel lies in the output even when that shovel is below the reserve. The second case jams a full output for good (point 3 above).

Change:
- Only a pile with 2 or more lying pieces qualifies; the most numerous non-shovel pile wins as before. A pile at 1 is left for the worker who may need it.
- Remove the "a free shovel lies here" exception. The drop runs only while `shovelsOwed` is set, and that already means free shovels are at or below the reserve, so the lying shovel cannot be used; making room for a second one is the way out.
- Unchanged: never a reserved piece, never a shovel, one drop per `DropEvery` 250 ticks, only at `DropAt` 7 or more, through the pile's own removal 0x16F8D0 (`Native::dropFromPile`).

Code: `dropCandidate` in `native/offence-model.hpp`; the inspector string `if(out[i].good==31&&out[i].count-out[i].reserved>0)return -1;` goes away and a `>=2` condition replaces it.

Tests (`toolWatch`): the existing cases adjusted, plus {25:1, 18:1, 31:1 reserved} at 8 drops nothing; {25:3, 31:1 free} at 8 drops a pickaxe; {25:1, 27:1, 29:1} at 7 drops nothing.

Field check: pid 31460's end state would now drop a rod, the toolsmith makes the second shovel, recruitment takes both.

Risk: with every other pile at 1 nothing is dropped and the shovel waits for the round robin; that output holds at most 7 goods then, so the toolsmith is not blocked.

## 5. Fix 3: reserve room in the telemetry and the report

Problem: the report cannot tell a deliberate room (reserve = carriers - room while workers are missing) from a failed floor.

Change:
- A `room` column in the ledger (`native/economy-telemetry.hpp`, `Ledger::room`, written before the three list columns), filled from `reserveSeen[p].room` in `ledgerWrite` (`native/dark-tribe.cpp`). The writer already sets aside a file with another header.
- `tools/economy-report.py`: `room` optional (-1 in old files); the flag "reserve below keep" fires only when the reserve is below both the keep and carriers - room; the per-player summary prints the room.

Tests: `ledgerRows` expected row and comma count (55 -> 56); the report on a synthetic file with room 4 must not flag.

Docs: the column table in `research/ECONOMY_TELEMETRY_0215.md`.

## 6. Fix 4: a winning land defence keeps going

Problem: a land-only defence (`c.landOnly`) ends after `LandDefendTime` 3,000 whatever it achieves (`offence-model.hpp` line of the `startReturn(... "defence time")` call). Six land-only defences in 18584 ended on that limit; the 16-gardener one at 105,570 was winning.

Change:
- When the limit is reached, extend by `LandDefendStep` 1,500 while the last step reduced own Dark by at least `LandDefendGain` 40 cells or still sees units, up to `DefendTime` in total. It still ends when the land is clean or the escort is beaten.
- The campaign keeps `landOnly`; the extension is a transient counter in `Campaign` (no `dt::State` field).

Code: the time check in the campaign step (`offence-model.hpp`, the `LandDefendTime` branch), a new constant pair next to `LandDefendGap`, `Campaign::landStep` and `Campaign::landOwnAt`.

Tests: a new `defenceLength` test with the fake engine: own Dark falling 60 per step keeps the defence to `DefendTime`; own Dark flat ends it at 3,000; a clean land ends it earlier.

Risk: a longer defence delays the next attack by at most `DefendTime` - 3,000 ticks; it only runs while it is winning.

## 7. Fix 5: a fast rise skips the attacks-first gap

Problem: `attackable()` defers a land-only defence until `LandDefendGap` 12,000 after the first attack time or the last land defence, even for a rise of `SeriousRise` or more per scan pass.

Change:
- A serious threat whose `rise >= SeriousRise` skips the gap (the deferral keeps applying to old Dark land with no rise, which is what protects The Alliance's start).
- Logged as `start: serious rise` so the two paths can be told apart.

Code: the `t<since+LandDefendGap` branch in `attackable()`; `defendNote` reason text; `inspect-offence.py` strings.

Tests: in `landFirst`, a rise of 40 in a pass starts a defence inside the gap; a rise of 0 with 500 own Dark cells still waits.

Risk: the Dark player greening fast near the computer triggers a defence sooner; that is the intended behaviour and it still takes only `escortSize` soldiers.

## 8. Fix 6: the defence aims at the land being lost

Problem: `assess()` puts the defence area on the unit cluster whenever units are seen; reclaimable own land is used only when no units are seen. pid 31460: 111 soldiers to two Dark Gardeners 118 tiles away while the land was lost elsewhere; pid 18584: the recall at 58,350 went to (420,221) while own Dark rose 1,412 to 2,135.

Change:
- When own Dark is rising (rise > 0 or own above `SeriousCells`) and the unit cluster is more than `DefendFar` 60 tiles from the centre of the reclaimable own Dark land, the defence area is that land, not the units; the soldiers still answer units that come within `DefendReach` of the area.
- A separate split (soldiers to units, gardeners to land) is not done in r50: two moving groups need their own convoy and fire handling.

Code: `assess()` area selection; a `DefendFar` constant; the `OFFENCE_DEFEND` line gets `landAt=` so the choice is visible.

Tests: in `defending`, two units 100 tiles from a rising own-Dark patch make the defence go to the patch; the same units next to the patch keep the unit area.

Risk: a few Dark units far from the land are left alone until they come close; the attacks on the Dark base are unchanged.

## 9. Fix 7: a gardener home guard instead of recalling campaigns

Problem: a campaign takes every gardener (`gardenersFor`), so nothing reclaims land at home while it runs. The reviewed proposal (recall raids, hunts and sweeps for Dark Gardeners or a rise) would re-create the r38 complaint "stops attack in the middle", because the Dark player's gardeners are near the computer's land most of the time.

Change:
- While own Dark is rising or above `SeriousCells`, a campaign may take at most `gardenersFor(style) - HomeGuard` gardeners, `HomeGuard` 4, never fewer than `MinGardeners`. The guard stays free for the existing reclaim pass, which already runs in every phase and sends free gardeners to own Dark land within `ReclaimRadius` of themselves or `LaunchReach` of home.
- No recall rule changes: only Dark fighters in the territory recall a running attack (r37).

Code: `gardenersFor` callers in the launch and top-up paths (`offence-model.hpp`); a `HomeGuard` constant; the reclaim pass unchanged.

Tests: `reclaiming` with a campaign running and own Dark rising keeps 4 gardeners at home and reclaims; `scaling` adjusted for the guard; the `convoySoak` counts.

Risk: a campaign leaves with 4 fewer gardeners while the home land is under pressure; the assault of 70 at 142,460 in 18584 had 12 and would have had 8.

## 10. Fix 8: diagnostics

Two observations of the review cannot be judged from the current logs.

- Fire keep (E): `assess()` drops Dark units within `HuntKeep` of a live fire centre before the threat is classified. Log, once per decision, `OFFENCE_DEFEND_FIRE player= dropped=<count> nearest=<Euclid> centre=x,y` when any unit was dropped, and add `beyondFire=1` to the `no way outside the fire defence` return. No keep or routing change until that log shows a hidden threat.
- Far gardeners (G): every 3,000 ticks, for each gardener outside `LaunchReach` of home, `OFFENCE_GARDENER_FAR player= id= at=x,y job= distance= held=<campaign or none>`; plus the count in `OFFENCE_RECRUIT` as `far=`. The ledger already has the total per type.

Code: `assess()` and the defence return path for E; `offenceRecruit` and the gardener listing for G (`dark-tribe.cpp`); inspector strings for both lines.
