# Computer offence against the Dark Tribe (0.2.15 rounds r27 to r32)

Date: 2026-10-14/15. User request: the computer opponents never attack the Dark Tribe. Reverse-engineer the computer AI and make it play properly against the Dark Tribe: destroy every Mushroom Farm first, then the Temple/Fortress, and win. Follow-up: Dark buildings must fall the native way, by Gardeners turning the ground around them green, so the computer needs an army that attacks and Gardeners that take back the land.

Offline static analysis only: no game was started, attached, captured or injected. Gameplay is unverified until the user tests it (TESTING.md, r27). Private notes and the 400+ instruction checks of the four analysis rounds are in `work/native-analysis/ai/` (ignored folder). `tools/inspect-offence.py` re-checks every native fact the plugin relies on (`research/offence-native-checks.json`).

## Round r32: scaled styles and protected convoy (as implemented)

User requests (2026-10-19):
1. "Make all styles grow so it will vary across all styles."
2. A screenshot from the user's game: the soldiers had already reached a Mushroom Farm while their Gardeners trailed far behind, unprotected. "Make it so they move all together, protecting them."

Source only: nothing was built, staged or tested in game when this section was written. Startup marker `; scaled styles and protected convoy r32`.

**Why the soldiers arrived without their gardeners (read from the r28-r31 code).**
- Gardeners were taken from anywhere and set off from wherever they were working. In the soak test they started 63 tiles from the army.
- A march leg ended once 70 % of the soldiers had arrived. Nothing checked the gardeners.
- For a target within 40 tiles, the gardeners got no order at all until the soldiers held the target. Then they walked there alone.
- Replacement gardeners, and the gardeners a hunt took afterwards, were sent straight from home.
- On the way home, the army was released as soon as the soldiers got home, wherever the gardeners were.

**Scaled styles** (`native/offence-model.hpp`: `raidSize`, `huntSize`, `escortSize`, `gardenersFor`, `gardenersWanted`, used in `decide`).
- *Army:* army = free soldiers − max(2, free/6), as in r28. Assault, pincer and allied still take the whole army.
- *Raid:* a third of the army, at least 10 (`RaidMax`).
- *Hunt:* half the army, at least 16 (`HuntMax`).
- *Sweep escort:* a quarter of the army, at least 8 (`SweepEscort`).
- None of them takes more than the army.
- *Gardeners per style:* the r28 number (raid 4, hunt 2, sweep 10, allied 6, assault/pincer 8) plus one per 15 soldiers the style takes (`GardenerPer`).
- *Cap:* `gardenersWanted(free soldiers)` = 12 + one per 10 soldiers above 40, at most 20 (`MaxGardeners`, which was 12 before). A launch counts the sector's free soldiers including the home reserve. Gardener recruitment and the r29 toolsmith shovel orders use the same number for the whole computer: the free soldiers of all its sectors plus the soldiers its running campaigns took (`offenceRecruit` in `dark-tribe.cpp`).
- Style weights are unchanged. With 144 free soldiers, every style is still chosen at least 5 times in 150 decisions (test `scaling`).

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 12 | 10 | 10 | 8 | 4 / 2 / 10 / 8 / 6 | 12 |
| 30 | 36 | 10 | 16 | 8 | 4 / 3 / 10 / 10 / 8 | 12 |

A launch takes no more gardeners than are free. An allied player without free gardeners still joins as an escort.

**Convoy rules** (`convoy`, `together`, `advance`, `gather`, `rearPoint` and the siege, sweep, hunt and adoption code in `offence-model.hpp`; constants `ConvoyLead` 10, `ConvoyGap` 8, `ConvoyBehind` 3, `ConvoyEvery` 30, `ConvoyStall` 600, `RallyGap` 30, `GatherHome` 30, `TopUpReach` 20, `EscortRadius` 16, `RearStep` 4, `ScreenRing` 6, `ThreatRadius` 30, `GuardWait` 300).
- *March:* every 30 ticks the army is ordered to the point of its leg 10 tiles ahead of the convoy's rear. The rear is whichever is farther from the leg's end: the army centre or the gardeners' centre. Each gardener gets its own order (one id per order, FORWARD) to a cell around the point 3 tiles behind that. The army takes the next point only along a clear leg; otherwise it goes straight to the leg's end if that leg is clear, and otherwise it plans again.
- *Waiting:* while any held gardener is farther than `convoyLimit` = 8 + the formation spread from the army centre, the army gets no new forward order and the gardeners are called to it. After 600 ticks of waiting, every gardener still that far is left behind: it is dropped from the campaign and sent home (respecting the fire keep-off). If that leaves a march with no gardeners at all, the army goes home (`gardeners left behind`). The target is avoided only if the army had already left home (farther than 30 tiles).
- *Fire first:* an army inside `soldierKeep(N)` of a fire centre never holds or steps short. It leaves by its exit leg directly, and the gardeners follow.
- *Legs:* a waypoint, the staging cell or home counts as reached only when the convoy is together. For a pincer, that is the first half. A short direct assault, without staging, is allowed only if the convoy is already together.
- *At the target (the screen):* the soldiers stand within 6 tiles of the door, on the side of the nearest Dark fighter within 30 tiles of the door, outside the fire keep. The gardeners wait at the rear point, 4 tiles from the screen on the far side from that fighter. With no threat, the rear point lies towards the army's approach. A waiting gardener that strays more than 16 tiles from the army, or stands nearer the threat than the screen, is called back to the rear point. Gardeners get ring cells only when the screen holds, or 300 ticks after the assault began if the army is within 16 tiles of the door. Never while Dark fighters are near the door.
- *Replacements:* top-ups come only from free gardeners within 20 tiles of the army, along a clear leg. The others stay home for the next convoy. A detached escort for distant gardeners was rejected: it would weaken the soldiers at the target and add another state.
- *Sweep:* the front moves only while the escort holds it. Gardeners work Dark land within 16 tiles of the escort and walk with it to each new position. While Dark fighters are near the front, they wait 8 tiles farther back, away from the front.
- *Hunt:* the hunt itself takes no gardeners, as in r28. Afterwards, gardeners are taken nearest the army first. If their centre lies more than 30 tiles from an army that is out in the field, the army first walks to them (`rally to the gardeners`), then attacks with them.
- *Allied joins and the way home* follow the same convoy rules. A squad is handed back at home only once its gardeners are with it.
- *After a load:* an adopted squad picks up gardeners near it and marches with them. A squad stays a raid up to max(12, `raidSize` of the launch army). The launch army is estimated from the sector's free soldiers now plus the squad. `want` is capped by `gardenersWanted`, as at launch. With 60 free soldiers, a squad of 16 stays a raid and one of 30 becomes an assault.
- *All gardeners died on the march:* the target is avoided and the army goes home.
- Every gardener destination still goes through `gather`. That checks `GardenerKeep`, standable cells and a clear leg. So the r30 fire keep-off holds for every new order path.

**Logs.**
- New: `OFFENCE_CONVOY player= sector= phase= style= target= kind= door=x,y army= gardeners= gap= limit= left= wait= at=x,y reason=`, at most once per 300 ticks per campaign. Reasons:
  - `waiting for gardeners`: the army holds. `gap` is the farthest gardener from the army centre, `limit` is the hold distance and `at` is the army centre.
  - `gardeners left behind`: `left` is the number dropped after 600 ticks.
  - `rally to the gardeners`: `gap` is the distance to the gardeners' centre, `limit` is 30 and `at` is the rally cell.
- Every other campaign line (`OFFENCE_LAUNCH`, `OFFENCE_STAGE`, `OFFENCE_ASSAULT`, ...) now has `gap= wait=` before `reason=`.
- `OFFENCE_RECRUIT` and `OFFENCE_RECRUIT_WAIT` show `wanted=`, the scaled gardener cap.

**Limits (r32).**
- Staged on 2026-10-19 together with r31: the full `python tools/workflow.py` passed and the staged DLL contains `clean start maps r31` and `scaled styles and protected convoy r32`. Not verified in game.
- *Walking speed:* every settler type should walk alike, but what reads that byte was not traced. `SETTLER_BOWMAN_02` has no speed entry at all. The convoy does not rely on equal speed; the slower-gardener soaks cover that.
- *March speed:* the real ticks per tile are unknown. The convoy advances at most about 7 tiles per 30-tick step (a 10-tile lead ahead of its rear). If units walk faster than that, armies now march more slowly than in r28-r31.
- *Untested in game:* where the screen stands, the 300-tick greening fallback and dropping stragglers after 600 ticks.
- *Timeouts:* a march that reaches `MarchTimeout` (4,000 ticks) still assaults without the convoy together. In the soak, a waiting gardener could get up to 22 tiles from the nearest soldier.
- *Fire guard:* it can bench every gardener of a march. The army then goes on alone and relies on top-ups at the target. A broader "go home without gardeners" rule was tried and reverted, because it turned those benches into aborted campaigns.
- *Hunts:* while hunting, the army still moves without gardeners, because they join only for the building afterwards.

## Round r30: citadel fire keep-off and farm blockade (as implemented)

User decision (2026-10-19, binding): computers must not come or dig close to the Temple/Fortress before all Mushroom Farms are destroyed, and all soldiers and Gardeners must avoid the fire field in every attack style and on every path. The user also said: "farm can't be inside this field, so create a blockade which won't allow it to be there; also if any farm is at the beginning of a starting map, insta-destroy this farm so it won't mess up everything."

**Field evidence (r27/r28 logs, pids 17836, 5672, 15576, 10104; no saves loaded; fire centres from the generated maps).**
- *Confirmed fire loss.* First Dawn farm 634 (pid 5672, r27) is the start farm at position 337,282 (door 338,285). It lies Euclid 18.3 from the Temple's fire centre 341,302, inside the fire even when it is the last farm (radius 18, 25 %). The siege started at 74560; the gardeners fell 6→4→2→0 by 75820 (1,260 ticks) and the army went home with `no gardeners left`, ring 18/24.
- *Sweep losses at the zone edge.* Iron Kingdom (pid 10104, r28, 13-23 farms, radius 24 at 100 %): map 1 kept 10 gardeners while the front was at 53→37 from the centre, then lost all 10 with the front parked at 32-33 (t 9500-15270). Map 2 lost 9 (10→1) at 32-33 (t 9660-14580). The Alliance (pid 15576 map 2) player 3 sweep with fronts 32-36 from the centre lost 5-7→0 gardeners within 2,200 ticks. The escort was never beaten, which fits a fire that hits only units on Dark land. Cause: the r28 front kept r+7 (hex) from the centre, but the gardeners green within `SweepRadius` 10 of the front, and their native search reaches about 28 around themselves, so they ended up about 22-24 from the centre.
- *Not fire.* Iron Kingdom farm 778 (door 330,379, 44 from the southern centre 368,423): five attacks ended 120-190 ticks after the siege began against about 127 Dark fighters (`versus=254`). No target 44 or more from a centre shows a fire loss. *Unclear:* the Alliance attacks on farms 36-39 from the centre and the hunts at 31-34. Native soldiers chase Dark fighters 13/15 tiles, which can pull them into the zone.
- *Structural.* Start farms inside the 18-tile fire of a single farm: First Dawn 337,282, and Iron Kingdom 391,124 (12.8 from the northern Temple's centre 388,135) and 413,161 (25.5). In session 15576 the player also built farms 23 and 27 from the centre. Under the user's rule such a farm could only be taken by entering the fire.

**Native facts, corrected (file-only; an independent re-check confirmed them and fixed five details; pins in `tools/offence_pins.py`, checks in `tools/inspect-offence.py`).**
- *Who burns.* Only `CDarkTempleRole` (vtable 0xC59284) runs the fire 0x1458C0. Its tick (slot 3, 0x1455E0) calls it at 0x145686 and reschedules itself with `0x16D5D0(0x1F)`, so each building makes one pass every 31 ticks on its own timer. The role factory 0x13C7A0 gives that role to house kind 18 (`HOUSE_KIND_DARKTEMPLE`), which `buildingInfo.xml` assigns only to the Temple (50) and the Fortress (51). **The Manacopter Hall (80) has no fire:** `CManakopterHallRole` (vtable 0xC593D0, tick 0x147A20) makes aircraft only. A construction site has `CBuildingSiteRole` and no fire. A whole-`.text` scan finds three event-2 posts: 0xF442E (spells), 0x145B54 (the fire, the only one with 255 damage) and 0x194D9E.
- *Centre and radius.* The centre is the building position (+0x18) plus (1,2). n = `[0x10EF7AC + p*0x298]` is the owner's **ready Farm count** (generic counters: sites at `0x10EF620 + (p*0x53 + type)*8`, ready at `0x10EF624 + ...`). n ≤ 0: no fire. n = 1..3: radius 18/20/22, hit chance 25/50/75 %, at most 16/64/144 candidates per pass. n ≥ 4: radius 24, 100 %, no candidate cap.
- *Euclidean disc.* The pass walks spiral 0xD69968 (entries dx, dy, distByte, dir, sorted by Euclidean distance in X = dx − dy/2, Y = 0.866·dy) up to `word[0xD7CF3A + R*4]` inclusive: 1,164/1,422/1,746/2,082 for R = 18/20/22/24. The zone is the disc dx² − dx·dy + dy² ≤ R² (2,083 cells for R = 24) and reaches 20/22/25/27 **hex steps** between the axes. The r27-r29 model measured it in hex steps (radius, +2, +7), which under-estimated it by up to 3 steps there. The cut-off is fuzzy by about 0.1 (R = 20 and 22 leave out a few cells at 19.92 and 21.93).
- *Counter.* +1 at 0x136340 (0x1362F0, a site completes; the mod's own Farm completion calls it, so mod-built farms count) and at 0x13C4F8 (a building created complete). −1 at 0x13C769 (0x13C680, reached from CBuilding slot 4 0x1361D0, which Kill 0x16D030 calls at 0x16D1CB **on the destroy tick**) and at 0x13F08E/0x13F128 (owner change). Growing sites do not count; a destroyed farm stops counting at once, although the entity stays readable for about 32 ticks.
- *Victims* (loop 0x145A00..0x145A9C): the cell is walkable Dark land (`flags & 5 == 4`), has an occupant in the ground map 0x11630DC, and lies in the door's land sector. The occupant is alive and hostile (enemy mask `[0xEDCD54 + p*4]`, so allies and owner 0 are spared), class 1 (settler) or 4 (vehicle), with a non-zero selection group (`byte +0x14 & 0xF`: soldiers, gardeners, priests, pioneers, thieves and vehicles; carriers and workers have 0; the per-type values are unverified). Walking or standing makes no difference.
- *Hits.* Each candidate rolls on the logic LCG, nearest first. Beyond Euclid 16 a pass ends at the (L+1)-th far candidate, with L = 8..15 drawn per pass. A hit plays effect 0x4E and posts event 2 with 255 damage 6..21 ticks later (handler 0xE52C0 → 0x181780). That kills every ordinary unit (Dark race −1; only the campaign hero type 0x2C is capped). The victim dies wherever it is by then, so corpses can lie 2-3 cells outside the zone.
- *Visible flames ≠ kill radius.* 0x145BA0 draws min(3n, 40) flame effects (0x4E) on random cells of spiral band 16 only (indices 823..929, Euclid 15.1..16.0; visual generator `[0xE938AC]+0x2C`). **The flames always show at about 16 tiles; kills reach 18-24.**
- *Green land is safe, but gardeners stand on what they green.* A green cell (bit 0x04 clear) is never a candidate, so a green corridor is safe. A gardener, however, stands on the Dark cell it greens: the WORK end 0x1795BD releases its reserved cell (role +0x40/+0x44) and lightens its own position. So every Dark cell greened inside the zone exposes the gardener to rolls. The gardener search 0x179F80 runs 57 slices of 48 spiral entries (up to index 2,735, about Euclid 28 around the gardener), in practice nearest to the gardener. A gardener whose nearer Dark land is green therefore wanders up to 28 tiles, into the fire if that is the nearest Dark land left. The aimed cell is readable at role +0x40/+0x44 under job 0xAE (mode 0) and 0xAF (mode 1).

**Keep-off rules (plugin).** The shared geometry is `native/citadel-fire.hpp` (namespace `dtfire`, pure), used by the offence director and by Dark construction. Every distance is Euclidean, `fire2(x,y,cx,cy) = dx² − dx·dy + dy²` against K², from each fire centre (position + (1,2)). The rules always use the native maximum radius, whatever the farm count: a farm completing during a march adds 2 at once, and the boundary is fuzzy.
- *Fire on:* the native ready-farm counter is above 0, **or** the director lists a ready farm, **or** a Farm construction site exists (the largest wins, fail safe; a site completes within about 40 ticks). Centres: every Temple and Fortress, ready or site. `OFFENCE_FIRE_STATE` logs a change.

| Constant | Value | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| `FireReach` | 24 | the native maximum kill radius |
| `FireLine` | 27 | no computer unit may stand within it; no gardener aims within it |
| `GardenerKeep` | 28 | gardener destinations, ring cells and beside cells |
| `soldierKeep(N)` | 27 + spread(N) | soldier destinations (stage, assault, waypoints, escort hold, exit points); spread = 2k+2 for the group order's formation slots (click + 2·spiral) |
| `legKeep(N)` | 27 + spread(N) + 3 | straight legs (the design's spread/2 let soak soldiers reach 25.5 between waypoints) |
| `FarmLock` | 33 = 27 + 4 + 2 | a farm closer than this is never targeted (its ring reaches 4) |
| `HallLock` | 44 = 27 + 15 + 2 | the same for a Hall (its ring reaches 15) |
| `HuntKeep` | 42 = 27 + 15 | a hunt ignores Dark fighters this close (soldiers chase 13/15 tiles) |
| `FrontKeep` | 37 = 27 + 10 | sweep fronts (the gardeners green `SweepRadius` 10 around them) |

- *Removed:* `FireMargin`, `FireRaidFarms` (its exemption let raids into the fire of 1-2 farms, which still burns 25-50 % per pass out to 18/20) and the hex-based `zones`/`exposure`/`detour`.
- *Targets:* while the fire is on, the Temple and the Fortress are locked, and so are farms within `FarmLock` and Halls within `HallLock`; locked targets are never chosen. A computer with only locked farms declines (`farms only inside the fire defence`). With the blockade below, a locked farm can only come from a save made before r30, and the load sweep removes those.
- *Routing:* `legClear` is exact. It checks the whole hex parallelogram between a leg's ends (every shortest hex path, no centre inside), not samples. `hexLeg` splits a leg at a parallelogram corner into two single-path legs along hex axes. `route()` is a shortest-path search over waypoints in 24 directions at four radii around each centre (each keeping `soldierKeep(N)`), with `HopCost` 20 tiles per stop and up to `MaxVias` 12 stops. An army starting within `GardenerKeep` first walks to an exit point (on the ray from the nearest centre, then rays turned by ±30°...). Staging tries the line from home, then lines turned ±30° to ±120° around the door. An assault cell must keep `soldierKeep(N)`; without one the target is avoided (`no safe assault cell`) and the army re-targets, at most 3 levels deep.
- *Way home:* `homeward()` routes around the fire too. Without a route the army walks to its exit point or holds where it stands and plans again every 60 ticks (`no way home outside the fire defence, holding`, logged once). It is never sent straight home. A squad is not handed back while a member is within `FireLine` until 12,000 ticks have passed (a stuck-forever cap).
- *Guard (every director step, 10 ticks, while a campaign runs and the fire is on):* a citadel target, or one the fire now locks, is left (`fire defence back on`). Every soldier within `FireLine` gets an order to the exit point. The gate is per soldier (32 slots of uid and time): a soldier that turns hot is ordered out in the same step, and one with no way out is retried after 30 ticks. A march or a way home then plans again from where the army stands. Extraction episodes count as lures per target, sweep front or phase, in March, Assault and Return. At 3 lures within 600 ticks the target is avoided, the march or front is given up, or the way home holds outside the fire (`lured toward the fire defence`). Held gardeners whose own cell or aimed cell (role +0x40/+0x44 under job 0xAE/0xAF) lies within `FireLine` are recalled beside their ring cell, to the army or out. A second recall within 600 ticks benches them (dropped and sent home).
- *Free gardeners (reclaim):* every step (was every 30 ticks), every free gardener, idle ones too, whose own cell or aimed cell lies within `FireLine` walks out. Reclaim never sends one into a `GardenerKeep` circle.
- *Sweep:* fronts keep `FrontKeep`. While the fire is on, a front advances towards the nearest unlocked farm, not towards the citadel. The escort's hold cell keeps `HuntKeep`, and lures at a front are counted.
- *Hunt:* Dark fighters within `HuntKeep` are ignored, and the army follows a group only along a clear leg.
- *Siege:* ring and beside cells within `GardenerKeep` are never given out; a ring with only such cells is avoided (`ring inside the fire defence`). A gardener is sent only along a clear leg. `clearFootprint` never moves soldiers onto a cell within `FireLine`.
- *Native AI soldiers* (not in a mod squad) within `FireLine` are only logged (`OFFENCE_FIRE_NATIVE`, at most every 300 ticks per player), never moved.

**Farm blockade (Dark construction; `native/blockade-model.hpp`, `construction-model.hpp`, `placement-native.hpp`; details in `CONSTRUCTION_INPLACE_0214.md`, r30).**
- A Mushroom Farm position must lie at least `FarmLock` 33 (Euclid, strict) from the fire centre of every own Temple and Fortress, completed or site. A Temple/Fortress site is refused when an own Farm, completed or site, would lie within 33 of its centre. The Hall is never blocked. Offence and blockade use the same `dtfire::farmTooClose`, so no farm can stand where the computers may not besiege it.
- Discovery refuses a blocked site with mode 1 before payment (`ConstructionEvent::FireBlocked`, never charged) and says *"Too close to the fire of your Temple or Fortress."* (Farm) or *"Too close to your Mushroom Farms. The Temple's/Fortress's fire needs room."* The placement hints never draw a green marker on a blocked anchor (`PLACEMENT_SCAN ... fireBlockade=`).
- *Start-map farms:* on the new-map tick (`initialize()` with `fireStartPending = !restored`), every own Farm inside a fire field is crushed once through the player's own demolish path (S4ModApi `BuildingsCrush` → Lua CrushBuilding 0x1C4D4C → mode-1 removal 0x136960 → Kill 0x16D030(id, 0) → slot 4 → 0x13C680, which lowers the ready counter at once). With attacker 0 and farm byte +0x25 = 0, no computer is credited. On that tick a Temple/Fortress wins over a Farm.
- *Load sweep (review fix):* on a restored save the first construction pass runs the same sweep once (`reason=load`), so a pre-r30 save cannot keep the citadel unbreakable. An r30 save holds no such farm, so the pass is idempotent and needs no schema change.

## Round r29: shovels from the computers' own toolsmiths (as implemented)

User request: the computers' economies should make shovels when Gardeners are missing. **Native facts (file-only; `tools/inspect-offence.py`, pins in `tools/offence_pins.py`).**
- A toolsmith makes nothing unless a tool is ordered. The consumer sub_14CE20 (0x14D008, building type 0x15) goes round robin over the economy's tool list from the building's last index. It takes the next tool whose order byte is above 0, decrements that byte unless it is 100, and otherwise produces nothing.
- The order is net event 0x13A9 (CEcoManager::SendProduceOrder 0x50A70, CEcoManager vtable slot 0x10C; wparam building<<16 | good, lparam amount). The handler at 0xF687D resolves the entity (0xF6120), the ready flag 0x2000000, role kind 4 (CProductionBuildingRole, slot 28 returns 4) and the economy (0xF6200), then calls 0xD6A90(eco, good, amount) for a type 0x15 toolsmith.
- In 0xD6A90 the tool list is the byte vector eco+0x354..+0x358 and the order counts are the parallel byte vector eco+0x348. Amount 0 stores 0, 100 stores 100 (continuous), and any other amount is added and clamped to 0..100. Both vectors are saved with the economy (0xD3F02..0xD3F83).
- The AI's CBuildingManagerTask sub_249400 sends two shovel orders (good 0x1F). The first, at 0x249835, is for its own diggers: 7-d or 10-2d, only while d < 5 and no shovel is in stock, so it is always positive.
- The second, at 0x249744..0x249770, can CLEAR or LOWER the order. It runs while a task is a built toolsmith waiting for its smith (+0x54 0x15, +0x30 3, +0x10 2, +0x14 != 3) and the task holds a toolsmith id [ebx+0x5c], which comes from the economy's ready toolsmiths and so includes start-content ones. Its amount is (10-d)-d, where d = vt+0x98(2) = word eco+0x28, the digger count (0x249422, 0x4F480).
- The AI recruits diggers while d < 10-d (0x2497A8), so its steady state is 5 diggers. There the amount is 0 and 0xD6A90 clears the order. From 6 diggers the amount is negative and is added, then clamped at 0 (0xD6B08). Gardeners (type 50) are recruited only by the plugin (0xD5100), so their shovels were never replaced.
- Tick order: the S4ModApi tick hook site 0xE9B18 comes before the net loop's event fetch (0xEF440) and dispatch (0xE9B5E), which come before the entity update.

**Plugin.**
- `offenceTools`, every 250 ticks inside `offenceRecruit`, uses `shovelOrder`/`shovelTarget` in `native/offence-model.hpp`. While gardeners are missing and free shovels are down to the reserve, it tops the home economy's order byte up to the shovels still needed, at most 4 (`ShovelBatch`), through 0xD6A90.

- `offenceShovelGuard` runs every tick with the transient `ShovelWatch`, which holds the plugin's part of the order. After a drop of two or more between two looks it puts that part back; a drop of one is production. A drop from 1 to 0 counts as production, and the 250-tick evaluation repairs that case.

## Why the computer never attacks the Dark Tribe (confirmed)

- **Target list.** Each sector AI (`CAINormalSectorAI`, one per land region holding the AI's towers) owns an enemy-building iterator set to building types 0x2E..0x30 only (small tower, big tower, castle; 0x4281D..0x42869). `EvalOffence` (0x42050, every 10 AI ticks) fills the offence goal caches from it, and `CAIAgentAttack::CheckStrengthAndFindAnyTarget` (0x2F3A0) picks only from cache +0x5A8. A Dark player owns none of these types, so the cache stays empty and the agent logs "No attack" (0x2F41E..0x2F429).
- **Second filter.** Every other target search (squad re-targeting 0x1B1610, suicide missions 0x43BC0, the global suicide mission 0x33210, squad attack states via 0x35320) requires selection group nibble 0xC on the target. The building constructor sets it only for house kinds MILITARY (10) and CASTLE (15) (0x135D08). Dark buildings are kinds 0x12 (Temple/Fortress), 0x14 (Farm) and 0x15 (Hall).
- **No native damage path.** Soldiers never damage buildings; they capture military buildings by killing the door unit (type 0x45, group 13). Catapults (0x1614B0) and warships (0x192800) filter targets through 0x198180, which rejects group-0 buildings. Axe warriors and saboteurs search buildings through 0x197FD0, which skips race 3 explicitly (0x198097). The Viking building spell skips Dark buildings (0xF4340).
- **Lua.** `AI.AttackNow(p, dark, n)` (0x3D2D0) posts event 0x1B; the sector calls 0x2F1E0, which reaches the same empty cache. The normal AI's gardener agent (`CAIAgentGardeners`, Execute 0x31740) is a stub (`xor eax,eax; ret 8`).
- **What still works natively:** soldiers fight Dark Warriors (type 29, group 2), Throwers (32, group 3), Shamans (54, group 4) and Dark Gardeners (52) automatically within about 13 tiles (`CWarriorBehavior` 0x198610). Mushroom Farmers (group 1) are never attacked.

## How the native game defeats Dark buildings (confirmed)

- Generic destroy 0x136A80 (`__thiscall`, ECX = building, one stack dword = destroying player, `ret 4`): collapse effect, inhabitant ejected (slot 25, economy reads null-guarded; the unguarded economy notify is skipped for race 3 at 0x136C4F), footprint cells cleared (Dark bit kept), "building lost" notice, deferred deletion 0x16D030 (unlinks from the per-player list and decrements the ready counter at once), then role slot 11. The Farm's slot 11 (0x145050) adds 1 to `Statistic.MushroomFarmsDestroyed(attacker)` when the attacker is non-zero.
- Only a normal-race Gardener greening the walkable ring of a Dark building calls it with a player (CGardenerRole 0x1795FF -> CDarkTribe slot 4 0xCBC80 -> 0xCBB60 -> 0x13CE10 -> 0x136A80(p)). Territory loss never destroys Dark buildings (0x13CE10 with p = 0 skips race 3).
- **The native "farms first" rule** is the Temple/Fortress fire defence 0x1458C0 (from the role tick 0x1455E0, one pass per 31 ticks): it sets fire to enemy settlers on walkable Dark land of the Temple door's sector within radius min(24, 16 + 2 x readyFarms), hit chance 25 % per ready farm, 255 damage. The radius is Euclidean (spiral 0xD69968), so it reaches up to 27 hex steps between the axes, and only the Temple and the Fortress have it, not the Hall (corrected in r30; see Round r30). It reads the owner's ready-farm counter `[0x10EF7AC + p*0x298]` and is off with 0 ready farms. Farms have no defence. So natively the citadel is unassailable while farms stand, and every destroyed farm weakens it. The mod's own Farm completion runs 0x1362F0, which increments that counter (0x136340), so mod-built farms count.
- Native loss detection 0x13EFD0 counts finished Temple/Fortress (role +0x1D). The scenario script `DT_Victory` declares the Dark player lost when `Buildings.Amount(1, 50|51, READY)` is 0.

## Design (as implemented)

The plugin cannot (and must not) make the native target filters accept Dark buildings: two independent filters, no damage path and no attack order exist. Instead each computer sector AI gets an offence director on the game tick (`native/offence-model.hpp`, pure and unit-tested by `offence-tests.cpp`; engine bridge `native/offence-native.hpp`; adapter `OffenceOps`/`offenceTick` in `dark-tribe.cpp`). It uses the AI's **own soldiers, own gardeners, own configuration and own decision rules**; Dark buildings fall to the gardeners' native greening. No units, resources or waves are created by the plugin (the maps give each computer player a few authored gardeners at the start; more are recruited natively). A design review (three independent lenses: native safety, gameplay on the three maps, plugin interplay; notes `work/native-analysis/ai/review-*.md`) changed several points before implementation; they are folded in below.

### Army: a mod-owned native squad
Verified in rounds 2 and 3 and by the native-safety review (`r2-squad-control.md`, `r3-squad-formation.md`, `r3-pools-parking.md`, `verify-r3.md`, `review-native-safety.md`):
- Player AI `[0x106B148 + 0xC + 4p]` (bit p of `[CAIMain+8]`, set by `bts` at 0x3856A; normal AI: byte +8 == 0, vtable 0xC2AA0C), sectors at +0x4C (count +0x48, vtable 0xC2B218). Free soldiers are members of the sector's per-group reservoirs ("pools") `[sector + 0x1C + 4g]` (vtable 0xC2A950) for groups 2..5; guard squads are `CAITaskForceSquad` (vtable 0xC2A91C, type 2, state 5) in the list `[sector + 0xC8]` (next +0x24). Reservoirs never move their members. With towers, almost every free soldier sits in a guard squad, so the army is taken from the pools first and then from guard squads (as safe as members dying; empty guard squads are deleted by CAIAgentSquads).
- Soldier filter: owner p, field-ready `(flags & 0x2008000) == 0x2000000` with flag 0x2000 (moves accepted), not dying, group 2..5, member record `[entity+0x10]` owned by a pool or a state-5 squad of the same sector. Never the tower-manning reservoir +0xA00 or the busy reservoir +0x170.
- **Raise:** `tf = 0x45020(state 6)` (thiscall, ECX = manager = sector + 0x58, `ret 4`; the factory native AI.NewSquad and the Dark squad manager use), `Command(0, 0, 0)` (vtable slot 8, 0x46880, `ret 0xC`), then the squad's AddMember (vtable slot 5, `ret 8`, returns 3) per soldier, detaching it with the native counters.
- **Untouched by the AI:** every native agent selects squads by state (Squads/IntruderCombat/refills 5, MovingHome 3, suicide 4) or by command (attack agent 3/4). A state-6 squad with command 0 is moved by nothing; its tick in sub-state 0 never orders members. Every tick the bridge re-derives player AI, sector and squad (list walk, vtable, type, state 6, command 0, manager) before any call; player AIs can be deleted at run time.
- **Movement:** `SendWarriors` (event 0x1396 -> `CGroupMgr` slot 10 = 0x16A800, which has no owner test; send gate `TribeTable[event player]+0x34`, so the local player 1 is passed), FORWARD for march, assault and the way home (WATCH would leave soldiers passive at home), at most 100 soldiers per order. Destinations never carry flag 0x01, so no soldier is garrisoned. The native squad mover is not used (its eco-sector path may fail on Dark land, and an out-of-map waypoint can hang 0x46370).
- **Give back:** walk home, then the native dissolve CAIAgentSquads uses (0x33C15..0x33C3A): `Command(0,0,0)`, `0x3FA50(sector, tf)` (members back to their pools), then the deleting destructor (slot 2) only when the squad is empty (a squad deleted with members loses them for good: load never re-runs the AI's adoption step).
- **Save/load:** the AI saves task forces with state and command, so a mod squad comes back as state 6 / command 0 (squad order reverses, members keep theirs). On the first tick after a load every such squad is adopted again (several per sector: the first is adopted, the rest dissolved); a raid-sized one (12 or fewer) stays a raid (r32: up to max(12, the raid size of the estimated launch army), and the adopted squad picks up gardeners near it). No sidecar change: schema 2 is unchanged. If the plugin stops at runtime (`fatal`, or a save that cannot be continued) every mod squad is handed back at once (`OFFENCE_RELEASE`), and a player that stops being an enemy gets its army back.

### When to attack: the native attack agent, mirrored
- Configuration from the AI's active column `[0xE92648 + p*0x17C]`: AttackMode (0 = never), AttackDelay (x 840 ticks), AttackChancePartial, AttackThresholdPartial/Total; `SquadMinFighters`/`SquadMinExtraFighters` (0xD7D10C/0xD7D118). Scenario `AI.SetPlayerVar` calls keep working.
- Timing as `CAIAgentAttack` state 0 (0x2FAF0): first check 512..639 ticks after the reset, never before AttackDelay x 840; chance 0x51E/65536 growing by 0x51E per failure up to 18 %; failures retry after 512 or 768 ticks; the reset after an attack restores both. The plugin uses its own random generator, never the game's (RVA 0xE66B10). F = Str = 0 (zeroed by the native reset after a load) means "not known yet": no failure is counted.
- Strength as `CheckStrengthAndFindAnyTarget` (0x2F3A0): the sector's F (+0xB1C, at least 15) and Str (+0xB20) from CAIAgentSquads, against the Dark fighter value `E(1)+0x38` (2 per Warrior/Thrower).
  - **Total attack** (AttackMode >= 2, roll not partial): `Str*100/AttackThresholdTotal > E(1)+0x38`; the army is all free soldiers except a reserve of max(SquadMinFighters, 1/5), and only if that is at least a raid (12); otherwise it becomes a raid. Total armies may take the citadel.
  - **Raid**: SquadMinFighters + SquadMinExtraFighters soldiers (12), keeping 3 at home. It compares its own weight (native weights 2/3/4 per level, 3..5 specialists, 10 squad leader) with the Dark fighters within 20 tiles of the target, `raid*100/AttackThresholdPartial > local`. Raids attack only Mushroom Farms and Manacopter Halls, never the Temple/Fortress, and never a farm in an active fire zone while 3 or more farms stand (r27; since r30 no style takes a farm within `FarmLock` 33 of a fire centre, whatever the farm count).
- Reachability: the target door must lie in the sector's land region (`[0xEF1A20 + 20*tile + 4]` == sector +0x100), as EvalOffence requires. On all three maps every Dark cell shares the AIs' land region (review: the tiling splits regions only at water, blocked cells and the map border).

### What to attack: farms first
- Targets come from the Dark player's per-type building lists `[0x10EEA74 + (p*0x53 + type)*4]` (next word +0x2A): completed (0x2000000), not dying (0x4000000), not deletion-marked, completed role vtable. Growing sites are never targets (the plugin would refund them).
- While any reachable ready Mushroom Farm stands, only farms are targets; then Halls, and the Temple/Fortress for total armies. Order (r27-r29): least fire exposure (how far inside a citadel's fire radius + 7, the siege ring), then nearest. Since r30 targets inside the fire are locked instead (`FarmLock`/`HallLock`; Round r30). Targets the army can beat come first (local Dark weight <= army weight x 100 / AttackThresholdPartial); a total army falls back to the least defended one. A farm completed during a citadel siege takes priority again.

### Fire defence
(r27-r29, superseded by Round r30: the zone is a Euclidean disc, every rule uses the native maximum 24 plus keeps, and routing is a waypoint search.) The fire zone of a ready Temple/Fortress is radius min(24, 16 + 2 x readyFarms) around its position + (1, 2) while the Dark player has ready farms. Staging cells stay outside every zone (+2); a march whose straight path crosses a zone that holds neither end gets a waypoint just outside it; the assault cell next to the door is the free one with the least exposure.

### Gardeners: how Dark buildings fall
The user's rule, confirmed natively: a Dark building is destroyed by Gardeners turning the ground around it green, and Gardeners take back Dark land. Facts (round 4, independently verified; `work/native-analysis/ai/r4-*.md`):
- **Collapse rule:** a Gardener (settler type 50, role `CGardenerRole`, vtable 0xC5B51C) greens one cell per WORK cycle (job 0xAF, about 31 ticks) through `CDarkTribe` slot 4 (0xCBC80) with its owner as the player. Greening a cell runs the ring walk (slot 7, 0xCBF40); when the walkable footprint cells around the blocked core ("ring": buildingPosLines minus blockPosLines, next to a blocked cell) are green, `0x13CE10(x, y, player)` destroys the Dark building through 0x136A80, credited to the gardener's player (`MushroomFarmsDestroyed` for farms). Ring sizes: Mushroom Farm 24, Temple 64, Fortress 66, Hall 66; a Farm can fall with its door cell still Dark. Territory never matters.
- **Blockers:** the gardener's cell test (0x179E70) skips cells with a unit or building id in the ground map, so any unit standing on a ring cell holds it Dark; an object or flag 0x40 on the last ring cell stops the native trigger.
- **The AI never uses gardeners** (`CAIAgentGardeners` is a stub) and never takes type 50 out of its pools (only Geologists 0x31 and Pioneers 0x2F are taken from pool 7), so gardeners stay in native bookkeeping; the plugin only orders them.
- **Orders:** a move order (event 0x11, command 0xD, FORWARD) makes the destination the gardener's work centre (0x179BA0); it then searches about 29 cells around itself and greens the Dark cells nearest that centre. WATCH parks a gardener for good, STOP sends it to cell (0,0): never used. One gardener per order (a group order gives each a formation slot as its centre). A gardener that finds nothing for 57 slices takes its idle default job (job 50).
- **Danger:** gardeners have 25 HP and never flee; Dark Warriors/Throwers attack them (after soldiers), and the Temple/Fortress fire defence burns them on Dark land while the Dark player has ready farms.

**Campaign with gardeners.** An attack needs at least 2 free gardeners in the sector's region (decline "no gardeners"); a raid takes 3, a total attack 6, nearest to home. They follow the soldiers to the staging cell (one group order), where they start greening nearby Dark land on their own. (r32 replaced this with the convoy: one order per gardener, legs end only with the gardeners together with the army, and they wait behind the screen until it holds; see Round r32.) At the target the soldiers hold a free cell at least 3 tiles off the footprint (they are not re-ordered while they hold it), and once the army has arrived, soldiers standing on a Dark ring cell are moved off it (at most every 60 ticks; units on ring cells keep them Dark, walking across the footprint is harmless). Once at least max(2, army/4) soldiers stand within 7 tiles of that cell and no Dark Warrior, Thrower or Shaman is within 10 tiles of the door, every gardener gets its own Dark ring cell, nearest to the army first, unoccupied first, with no Dark fighter within 10 tiles of it, and is sent beside it (never onto it). A gardener is left alone while its reserved cell (only meaningful in the greening job 0xAF: a gardener that gave up keeps a stale one) is a Dark ring cell, or while it walks to or works its still-Dark cell; it gets a new cell when its cell is green, when it is idle or has failed its search, or when its own search has drifted more than 4 tiles away from its still-Dark cell (the native search follows the green frontier; a review simulation showed 19 of 64 sieges stalling without this rule). A new target clears all assignments. Lost gardeners are topped up from free ones every 60 ticks (r32: only from free gardeners within 20 tiles of the army); with none left the army goes home ("no gardeners left"). The building collapses natively (`OFFENCE_COLLAPSE`). If the whole ring is green and the building still stands for 20 ticks, the plugin calls `0x136A80(building, attacker)` once every 30 ticks as a fallback (`OFFENCE_RAZE`, a refusal logged once), with the guards above. While a Temple/Fortress still parks entering settlers, the gardeners leave its last two ring cells alone (a native collapse would leave those settlers off the map), also while the area is not secure, and come back once the role has released them.

**Fire rule.** While the Dark player has any ready farm (also an unreachable one), the citadel's fire defence is on and no army targets the Temple/Fortress; Halls remain targets (since r30 only Halls outside `HallLock` 44, and the fire also counts as on while a Farm site exists).

**Reclaiming land.** After the attack delay, every 300 ticks, up to two idle free gardeners of each computer player walk to the nearest workable Dark cell within 40 tiles of them (and within 60 of the sector's home), unless it lies in a fire zone, a Dark fighter stands within 10 tiles, or it is within 35 tiles of a Temple/Fortress (only total attacks take the citadel) or of one of the player's paid or pending construction sites (`OFFENCE_RECLAIM`). They keep greening nearby Dark land on their own until none is within reach; that can also collapse a Mushroom Farm or Hall in reach. A reclaiming gardener that has crept within 35 tiles of a Temple/Fortress or site, or into a fire zone, is called home on the next pass.

**Supply.**
- *Start:* the maps give every computer player authored gardeners on free, non-Dark cells of its own land: First Dawn 6, Iron Kingdom 8, The Alliance 4 per people (builders `scenario-opponent.cjs`, `build-iron-kingdom.cjs`, `build-alliance.cjs`, checked by the `test-*.cjs` scripts).
- *Native recruitment:* every 500 ticks per computer player, the plugin orders one gardener through the player's own economy exactly as the specialist menu does (`0xD5100(eco, 0x32, 1)`; net message 0x13AF): within 16 ticks an idle carrier fetches a shovel (GOOD_SHOVEL 31) and becomes a gardener. It orders strictly one at a time: only when fewer than 6 gardeners are alive or pending, no order is pending (the order is additive) or on its way (carriers walking to a shovel are not in the per-player lists, so an order counts until a gardener more is alive or 1,500 ticks passed), more than 2 free shovels remain (pile stock minus each pile's reservations, as the native pile search 0x170B80 counts them; diggers and pioneers need shovels) and the economy has a spare idle carrier (`OFFENCE_RECRUIT`). The economy is the one at the sector's home cell (EcoSectorPool +6, AllEcoSectors 0x106C8A4, owner byte +0x12).

### Siege and march details
- March to a staging cell about 16 tiles before the target (walkable, unoccupied, no building/tower/blocked flag, the sector's region, preferably off Dark land), re-check a raid's local defence there (more than 1.5x what it can take: the target is avoided for 1,000 ticks and another one is chosen; total armies commit), then assault.
- Then the army re-targets from its position. It goes home when no target remains (a raid when no farm or Hall is left), after losing 60 % of its soldiers, with no gardener left, when a target is not reached within 9,000 ticks, or after 40,000 ticks; a dead order is logged once (`OFFENCE_ORDER_IGNORED`). Gardeners walk home with the soldiers.

### Hardening of existing code
A destroyed building stays readable for up to about 32 ticks until the deferred delete. `nearestBase` (aircraft "Return to base"), `templeQueues` and the panel's selected building kind now also require `standing()` (UID not deletion-marked, flag 0x4000000 clear). The heap-free diagnostics' `Text::put` is no longer inlined, so its literal log tags stay intact in the DLL (verify-offline checks them).

### Constraints kept
Ticks only, inside the state lock; no Lua; no new full entity scans (building/settler lists and AI lists only); `dtmem::invalidate()` after every native call; transient state; all native entry points pinned (`tools/offence_pins.py`, 67 relocation-aware regions since r28, startup return code 13) and re-checked by `tools/inspect-offence.py` (169 instruction checks since r28, plus vtable slots, tables and range scans); logs `OFFENCE_*`.

## Logs
`OFFENCE_DECLINE` (on a new reason and every 20th), `OFFENCE_LAUNCH`, `OFFENCE_STAGE`, `OFFENCE_ASSAULT`, `OFFENCE_SIEGE` (gardeners at work), `OFFENCE_GREENING` (every 8 ring cells), `OFFENCE_COLLAPSE` (native collapse), `OFFENCE_RAZE`/`OFFENCE_DESTROY`/`OFFENCE_DEFER` (fallback), `OFFENCE_GARDENERS` (replacements), `OFFENCE_RETARGET`, `OFFENCE_AVOID`, `OFFENCE_RETURN`, `OFFENCE_DISSOLVE`, `OFFENCE_LOST`, `OFFENCE_ADOPT`, `OFFENCE_RECLAIM`, `OFFENCE_RECRUIT`, `OFFENCE_ORDER_IGNORED`, `OFFENCE_ORDER_REFUSED`, `OFFENCE_RAISE_FAILED`, `OFFENCE_RELEASE`. Each campaign line names player, sector, phase, raid/total, target, kind, door, army size, gardeners, green ring cells/ring size, the strength values compared and the reason.

## Limits and risks (to be checked by the user)

- Everything is offline evidence. Whether the AI forms an army, marches, protects its gardeners, greens the rings and collapses buildings must be tested in game. Greening times measured in the user's sessions (`OFFENCE_SIEGE` to `OFFENCE_COLLAPSE`): r27 (pid 5672) 22 farms in 80-3,510 ticks, median about 1,470 with 6 gardeners and about 1,760 with 3; r28 (pid 15576) 29 farms in 680-2,540 ticks (median about 1,220) with 4-8 gardeners and one Temple in about 2,200 ticks with 8. The earlier estimate (300-600 per farm) was 2-6 times too short. The tick rate in those sessions varied from 14 to several hundred ticks per wall second (likely the game-speed setting, unconfirmed), so reason in ticks, not minutes.
- Units standing on ring cells (also the Dark player's own) keep those cells Dark; a building can then outlast the target time-out.
- Gardeners green the land around them on their own; a GROWING Dark construction site caught in that can still collapse (the plugin then treats it as lost and refunds its mana, existing rule). Reclaiming keeps 18 tiles from sites (r28; 35 in r27), attacks do not target them. Greened land stops Mushroom Farm planting there.
- **Soldier supply (map content, not plugin):** the review found that the AIs can barely replace lost soldiers (gardeners are recruited natively while shovels last). First Dawn's Vikings have a barracks but no weapons or weapon chain; the Alliance peoples have neither barracks nor weaponsmith; Iron Kingdom's Vikings have weaponsmiths but no barracks. First Dawn therefore gets about one raid, the Alliance two or three attacks per people, unless the AI builds a weapon chain and a barracks itself (unverified). Adding start weapons or a barracks to the maps would be a scenario change for a later round.
- First possible attack: AttackDelay x 840 ticks (Normal: First Dawn and Iron Kingdom 15, Alliance 20 game minutes), then a median of about eight checks (512..768 ticks each).
- The formation spread of `SendWarriors` near large footprints and the marching time across the map are not measured at runtime.
- A save that is loaded without the plugin keeps the mod squads in state 6: those soldiers stay idle and are lost to the AI (no crash path found).
- While an army is away the AI keeps only its reserve at home and may recruit more (SoldierLimit), as native attacks also allow.
- A Manacopter Hall destroyed by the computer refunds 300 mana per Manacopter still queued there (the existing Hall-loss rule).
